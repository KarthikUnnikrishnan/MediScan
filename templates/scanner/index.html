MediScan uses a fine-tuned Vision Encoder-Decoder Transformer (TrOCR). Traditional OCR looks for rigid letter shapes which fail with doctor cursive ligatures. TrOCR decodes characters in sequential visual context, matching recognized syllables against our local index of pharmaceutical names and active salts.
